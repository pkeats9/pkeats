Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

PK Eats ("we", "us") lets you order food, pay with a PK Eats balance and track your order at pkeats.com and in the PK Eats Discord server. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture. If you sign in with Discord: your Discord ID, username and avatar. We never receive your Google or Discord password.
  • Orders. The restaurant, items, prices, pickup or delivery choice, delivery address, driver notes and tip you enter, and the order number and tracking link.
  • Your balance. Deposits, order payments, refunds, credits and your current balance.
  • Deposits. The amount, payment method (such as Venmo or PayPal) and the payment code you include. To confirm a deposit we read the payment receipt sent to our business email, which can include the sender's name and the amount.
  • Technical data. A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in, and basic request logs used to keep the service secure and working.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your balance, orders and deposits together.
  • To place your food order and show you its status and tracking.
  • To confirm deposits, prevent fraud and fix problems with orders or payments.
  • To contact you about your orders or account.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Restaurants and delivery partners. To fulfil your order we pass the order details, your name or order name and, for delivery, your address and driver notes to the restaurant and delivery service, including Wonder.
  • Service providers that host and run PK Eats (such as our hosting and database providers), only as needed to operate the service.
  • When required by law or to protect the safety and rights of our customers and PK Eats.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and to make sign-in and cart building work. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, balance history and order records while your account is active and as long as needed for payment records, refunds and disputes. Sign-in sessions expire after 14 days.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, and you can ask us to show you, correct or delete your information by contacting us. If you have a remaining balance, we'll work with you on it before closing your account.

Children

PK Eats isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know on the website.

Contact

Questions or requests: pkeats9@gmail.com.
